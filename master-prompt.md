## Промпт для новых кадров

> Use case: photorealistic-natural. Reference image: Beşiktaş Bafra Pide menu photo, use it for exact kind of dish and natural proportions. Create one isolated photographic website asset. Single long boat-shaped OPEN Turkish pide. Make the visible filling uninterrupted along the center, edged with a naturally blistered thin baked rim tapering to points. Real photo art direction: warm soft window light upper left, 55-degree elevated view, left end slightly lower, true food color, dry crisp crust, gentle baked cheese highlights. A single low-profile restaurant serving board or plate following the reference. Whole composition visible, landscape 2:1, margins, clean genuinely transparent alpha outside the vessel. No table, text, shadows baked into background, hands, bowls, exaggerated garnish or cheese pull. The result must match a real baked Turkish pide, visually consistent with a premium menu.

- Beşiktaş Kıymalı Pide. Fill with a thin layer of seasoned browned ground beef flecked with minced beef pastırma and sucuk, with a little melted kaşar. Retain the browned meat-forward profile of the reference; no salad plate. Long oval wood board.
- Tavuklu Pide. Thin kaşar topping with distinctly recognizable natural pieces of chicken breast, small red tomato and green pepper accents and a little dried thyme. Serve on the simple white low-rim rectangular plate from the reference. Avoid dark beef, giant chunks and bright orange tint.
- Ispanaklı Kaşarlı Pide. Keep studded cooked chopped dark green spinach and pale melted kaşar visibly alternating across an OPEN boat. Narrow wooden board. No egg and no extraneous greens.
- Patatesli Kaşarlı Pide. OPEN boat, filled edge to edge with soft crumbled potato and lightly blistered kaşar, mottled pale golden yellow with a few naturally crisped brown spots. Retain narrow wooden board. No greens, meat, french fries or whole potato slices.
